The problem

Centralising data is the bottleneck.

Traditional AI moves all data to one server. For sensitive or large-scale data, that often isn't safe or practical.

Privacy & compliance risk

Moving sensitive records off-site creates breach exposure and regulatory hurdles.

Scale & cost

Large, distributed datasets are slow and expensive to move, and often too big for one server.

The federated alternative

The model travels to the data. Only model updates are shared, and raw data never leaves its owner.
