BankingCombined FL

Cross-institution fraud detection across four organisations

A retail bank, corporate bank, credit bureau and digital bank wanted a shared fraud model. Their customers partly overlap, each holds different features, and only two have fraud labels.

The challenge

Banking regulation rules out pooling customer data, even in encrypted form, yet much fraud spans institutions.

How it worked

  1. Private set intersection aligned shared customers without revealing their IDs.
  2. 40 federated rounds, with differential-privacy noise added before every upload.
  3. Each institution validated the global model on its own held-out data.

Galaxy classification across three survey institutes

Three institutes hold 5.5 PB of telescope imaging between them. The data shares one schema and labelling scheme, but each institute covers different regions of sky.

The challenge

The barrier was physical, not legal: transferring 5.5 PB would take months, and no single facility could train on all of it.

How it worked

  1. Each site ran an identical preprocessing pipeline, verified by comparing summary statistics, not images.
  2. 60 rounds of 3 local epochs each, uploading about 280 MB of model weights per round.
  3. FedProx let the smallest site skip up to 20% of rounds without destabilising training.
