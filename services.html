Services

From strategy to deployment.

Three stages, each useful on its own. Start where you are.

01
Readiness assessment

Is federated learning right for you?

We evaluate whether FL is practical, compliant and effective for your organisation before you invest.

  • Use-case discovery and stakeholder interviews
  • Map data sources and partitioning (horizontal, vertical or combined)
  • Feasibility: data heterogeneity, network and infrastructure
  • Privacy, security and regulatory requirements
Feasibility report Privacy recommendation
02
System design

A blueprint you can build.

We turn the assessment into a concrete technical architecture and an actionable plan.

  • Architecture: central coordinator or decentralised
  • Model selection and aggregation strategy (e.g. FedAvg, secure aggregation)
  • Roadmap with milestones, resources and cost estimates
  • Recommended frameworks, tools and platforms
Architecture blueprint Deployment roadmap
03
Deployment & support

From framework to production.

Where you need it, we take the design through to a working, supported system.

  • Framework setup (e.g. Flower, TensorFlow Federated, PySyft)
  • Model development and integration with your infrastructure
  • Privacy techniques, testing and performance tuning
  • Monitoring, documentation and team training
Production FL system Knowledge transfer
